Aircraft Checklist: Voice

Privacy Policy · app package com.baderkamal.aircraftchecklist

Effective date: 20 October 2026
Developer: Bader Kamal
Contact: baderkamal@gmail.com

In short: the app works offline and keeps your aircraft, checklists and run history on your device. It has no ads, no analytics and no tracking, and it does not sell or share your data. If you subscribe to Pro, your purchase is handled by Google Play and verified by RevenueCat.

1. What the app does

Aircraft Checklist: Voice reads aircraft checklists aloud (challenge and response) and lets you confirm each item by tapping or, where supported, by saying words such as “check” or “done”. It is an aid to situational awareness and is not a replacement for the official POH/AFM.

2. Data stored on your device

  • The aircraft you add, including any tail number you type in.
  • Your checklists and any edits you make to them.
  • Your checklist run history (which checklist, when it ran, and which items were confirmed or skipped).
  • Your settings, such as speech rate, voice and theme.

This data stays in the app’s private storage on your device. It is not sent to us. Uninstalling the app or clearing its data deletes it.

3. Microphone and speech recognition

If you turn on Voice confirmation and your version of the app supports it, the app asks for permission to use the microphone and speech recognition. It listens only while a checklist item is waiting for confirmation, and only to recognise a few command words (check, done, skip, back, repeat).

  • The app does not record, save or upload audio.
  • Recognition is performed by your device’s speech recognition service (for example Google on Android or Apple on iOS). Depending on your device and settings, that service may process audio on its servers under its own privacy policy.
  • You can deny or revoke the permission at any time; tap-to-confirm always works.

4. Text-to-speech

Checklist items are read aloud using the voices installed on your device. The text is spoken by your device’s text-to-speech engine; the app does not send it to us.

5. Checklist library

Aircraft checklist packs are built into the app. If a version of the app downloads packs from an online library, it only requests the packs; it does not send your personal data.

6. Optional account and sync

Some versions of the app may offer an optional Account & sync feature. It is off unless you sign in. If you sign in, your email address is used to send you a one-time sign-in code, and your aircraft, checklists and run history are stored with our hosting provider (Supabase) so they can sync between your devices. You can sign out at any time and ask us to delete your account data by email.

7. Exporting your log

If you use Export in the Logbook, the app creates a CSV file of your runs and opens your device’s share sheet. Where it goes next is your choice.

8. Subscriptions (Pro)

Pro is sold as a Google Play subscription. Payment is handled entirely by Google Play; the app never sees your card or payment details. To check whether Pro is active, the app uses RevenueCat, a subscription service that acts on our behalf. RevenueCat receives:

  • a random, anonymous app user ID created on your device (not your name, email or advertising ID);
  • your Google Play purchase records for this app (product, purchase token, dates, trial and renewal status);
  • basic technical details needed to validate the purchase, such as the app version, platform and store country.

This information is used only to unlock Pro, restore purchases and keep the subscription status correct. It is not used for advertising and is not sold. See RevenueCat's privacy policy and Google's privacy policy. You can cancel or manage the subscription at any time in Google Play.

9. What we do not do

  • No advertising and no advertising identifiers.
  • No analytics, crash-reporting or tracking SDKs.
  • No access to your location, contacts, photos, calendar, SMS or call logs.
  • No selling of personal data, and no sharing beyond the service providers described above.

10. Delete your data

Everything the app stores on your phone can be deleted by you at any time:

  • Logbook › Clear history removes your run history.
  • Uninstalling Aircraft Checklist: Voice, or Android Settings › Apps › Aircraft Checklist: Voice › Storage › Clear data, removes all aircraft, checklists, history and settings.

To delete the subscription record held by RevenueCat (and any account & sync data, if you used it), email baderkamal@gmail.com with the subject “Aircraft Checklist: Voice — delete my data”. Include the Google Play order number of your subscription (from your Google Play receipt) so we can find the record. We delete it within 30 days and confirm by email. Google keeps its own record of the purchase for its legal and accounting obligations; see Google’s privacy policy. Deleting this data does not cancel an active subscription — cancel it in Google Play first.

11. Children

The app is intended for pilots and is not directed at children under 13.
